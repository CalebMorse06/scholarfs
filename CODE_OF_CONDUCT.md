# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ScholarFS contributors should make participation safe, respectful, and useful. Be specific about ideas and behavior, assume good intent without dismissing impact, and leave room for people with different technical experience, educational backgrounds, abilities, identities, and access needs.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, publishing private student information, deliberately exposing credentials, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Student data deserves extra care

Do not ask contributors to share real grades, accommodations, schedules, instructor messages, paid course materials, authentication data, or identifiable workspaces to prove a bug. Use synthetic fixtures and redact screenshots.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's confidential **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and prefix the title with `Conduct:`. This shared private channel is used for both security and conduct reports; do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close threads, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed, and an uninvolved maintainer should review a report when possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents ScholarFS elsewhere.

This compact policy is inspired by the goals of the Contributor Covenant and may be replaced with a versioned community standard as governance grows.
